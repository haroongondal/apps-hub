Port authority approves night ferry service starting October

By Farah Siddiqui, City Desk

The metropolitan port authority voted 6–1 on Tuesday to approve a year-round night ferry service between the harbour terminals, ending a decade of stalled proposals.

The first crossings are scheduled for October 12, with departures every forty minutes until 2 a.m. Fares will match the daytime schedule.
