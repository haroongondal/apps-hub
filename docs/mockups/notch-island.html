3. Ongoing call

While a call is running, the island switches to the call, ahead of music and timers. Folded, it shows the call timer in green. Tapped, it shows the speaker and end controls.

What it showsThe call timer and the controls only. No caller name or message text: the listener reads the call's category and duration, not what is said or written.
NeedsThe owner's notification access for Notch, the same permission now playing needs. Without it, the island still shows the clock and battery, and calls are not detected.
  • Call takes priority over music and timers.
  • End call works through the call notification's own hang-up action.
  • Speaker is a toggle on that same action, when the phone supplies one.

What I'd build first

Option A for the expanded card, because it keeps the camera clear and fits the existing size settings. For calls, the folded green timer plus the tapped controls. Both sit under the same settings screen, and the call behaviour needs no new permission beyond the one now playing already needs.
